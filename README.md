## Found on the Oval

- **Welcome to Ohio State AR**

- **Live page: (https://julianabarros-7.github.io/geo-model-viewer-starter-main/)**

- **Coordinates: (lat: 39.999689, lon: -83.01113)**

- **Intent:
When choosing a location for our AR experience, we were immediately drawn to the center of the Oval. This area is one of the most recognizable and active spaces on campus, where students and faculty regularly cross the lawn, with Thompson Library and other significant university buildings visible in the background.

As we observed the space, we noticed several groups of prospective students taking campus tours. This inspired us to think about how our AR experience could showcase Ohio State pride in a location that represents the heart of the university. The Oval provides the perfect setting because it naturally attracts students, visitors, and prospective Buckeyes who are experiencing campus for the first time.

With this in mind, we chose to model the iconic Block O alongside a Buckeye, creating a simple but recognizable representation of Ohio State spirit. Placing these symbols in the center of the Oval allows our AR experience to celebrate university identity while connecting with the people who make this space so meaningful.**
